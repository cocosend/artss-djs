# CC0 audio assets

Sixteen drum one-shots from `octalmusic/octal-samples` (house-core pack):
https://github.com/octalmusic/octal-samples

The source repository explicitly publishes its owned synthesised samples under **CC0-1.0**, with a per-sample `LICENSES.md` audit. Original names and relative paths are retained under `samples/`. Files are copied unchanged. See `src/sample-library.js` for the exact inventory and bank assignments. The app's original sub-bass and acid synths remain separate and are generated locally.

No user-uploaded music is downloaded or analyzed. Assets are bundled in the same-origin PWA and pre-cached for offline playback. Samples can be freely used and redistributed under the stated CC0 dedication; provenance is retained as a transparency measure.
